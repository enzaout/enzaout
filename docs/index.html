Questions

Before you ask.

Isn't it weird to play a card game at 30?

For about ten seconds. Then someone answers a card, the whole table goes quiet, and nobody thinks it's weird anymore. Grown-ups need this more than kids do.

How many people can play?

Two or more. It's great one-on-one on a date and wild with a full table at a hostel.

How long does a game take?

As long as you want. Some people talk about themselves for 20 minutes straight (you know who you are). Tip: set a timer, or keep each answer to one minute.

What if I don't want to answer?

Then pass. It's totally fine. We'll just think a little less of you. 😘

Is it in English?

Yes. That's the point — you can play it with anyone you meet, anywhere in the world.
