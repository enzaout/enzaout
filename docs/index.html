Why I made this

I got sick of it.
So I did something.

I couldn't take one more “so what do you do?” Not one. I wanted to know people for real — their dreams, their fears, the stuff they never say.

So I printed a deck of questions. I walked up to a table of strangers in a hostel and said: “Wanna play a game?” By the end we were friends. I sold that deck on the spot. Then I printed more.

— The girl who hates small talk

Not magic. Science. In 1997, psychologist Arthur Aron showed that strangers who ask each other questions that get deeper, step by step, feel close in under an hour. That's how this deck is built.

Before

Isn't a card game weird at 30?

For ten seconds. Then someone answers a card, the table goes quiet, and nobody thinks it's weird anymore.

How many people can play?

Two or more. Great one-on-one on a date. Wild with a full hostel table.

How long does it take?

As long as you want. Some people talk for 20 minutes straight — tip: one minute per answer.
