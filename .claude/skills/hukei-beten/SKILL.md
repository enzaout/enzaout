## Iron rules (never break)
1. Listen to the body; nothing fits everyone.
2. **Spinach never with meat, chicken or fish.**
3. Reduce soluble fibre (sweet potato, broccoli, banana, raw carrot, avocado, figs, peaches, oranges, tofu, tempeh, asparagus, oats). Prefer insoluble: coconut, sorghum, millet, amaranth, rye, quinoa, peas, cooked spinach, pear, blueberries, walnuts, pine nuts, sunflower seeds.
4. Prebiotics are the base: onion, leek, garlic, Jerusalem artichoke, cucumber, sauerkraut, cooked-then-cooled starch.
5. **Never animal protein + starchy carb** (no fish+rice, steak+potato, burger bun, pasta bolognese). Bone broth counts as animal — never cook legumes/grains in it. Everything goes with non-starchy vegetables. Legumes + grains together = complete protein.
6. No vitamin supplements.

## Never use
White sugar, white flour, wheat, corn, soy/soy sauce, seed oils/canola, margarine, cow milk, yogurt, canned tuna, farmed salmon, hot spices, paprika, garlic/onion powder, spice blends, peanuts, roasted nuts, white salt, instant/roasted coffee. Sourdough bread is OK.

## How to cook
- Cooked > raw. Vegetables are **never fried**: steam, boil then oven, or straight to oven.
- Fats: tallow (best for heat), ghee, butter, coconut oil, cold-pressed olive oil (drizzle after).
- Nightshades (tomato, pepper): **only a little** — max ½ cup cooked per dish. Crucifers: small amounts, cook very well. Kale/spinach only cooked. Carrot only cooked. Sweet potato/potato only cooked-and-cooled, small, peeled.
- Legumes: **must sprout** (soak 4–24h → drain → rinse twice a day → wait for a 2–3 mm tail), long cook / pressure cooker.
- Grains: whole only (basmati, buckwheat, quinoa, millet), rinse well, soak overnight (min 1h). A little is fine, never the base of a meal.
- Nuts: soaked, max 1–2 a day.
- Spices fresh, seeds/leaves: turmeric (+black pepper +fat), cinnamon, cardamom, cumin, coriander, parsley, ginger, mint, basil, fennel, anise, clove, thyme. Few at a time, no blends.
- Salt: Atlantic / Himalayan / Celtic.
- One spoon of fermented veg (sauerkraut / pickled cucumber / carrot) with lunch and dinner.
- Meat: pasture-raised; organ meats once a week. Fish: wild salmon, sardines, herring, mackerel, trout, halibut, fresh tuna. Eggs: organic, **2–3 in the morning** (she felt 80% full after 2; sunny-side/poached eggs don’t keep, so cook only what she’ll eat).

## Cooking style — make it DELICIOUS
- **Omer Miller:** sear hard in tallow → same pot, whole garlic + onion → vegetable, lid on, no water, low oven for hours → uncover, 220° + grill 15 min.
- **Essy Roz:** "a minute of work" — sealed tray, 180° then 160°, uncover to brown.
- **Zohar Lustiger:** onion until golden, add roots/legumes + spices + water, simmer covered, fresh herbs + greens + lemon in the last 10 min.
- Always: deep browning on meat, whole sweet garlic, jammy onions, lemon + herbs at the end, something fresh/crunchy on top, coarse salt. Give each dish an appetizing name.
